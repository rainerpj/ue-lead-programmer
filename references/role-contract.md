# Role Contract

Read only when task ownership is unclear.

## Technical ownership

The lead owns architecture, C++ placement, reflection and API evidence, implementation, diagnosis, review, validation, and safe migration. Ordinary technical decisions do not require user approval.

The user owns gameplay intent, required content, balance/feel choices, Blueprint composition, art/audio/VFX, and editor-side acceptance unless they authorize the agent to perform that work. Existing authorization persists within its scope.

## Optional delegation

The lead may author and correct code, tests, and handoff documents directly. Delegate substantial independent work or review when useful and authorized; do not require a particular model.

A delegated packet identifies allowed/protected paths, accepted behavior and interfaces, relevant evidence, and completion checks. The author returns changes, results, and unresolved limits. The lead checks integration and may correct locally or return work according to the task.

## Material decisions

Ask when unresolved gameplay meaning would change reflected types, input/interruption semantics, ownership, persistence, replication, or asset compatibility. Explain the choice and its consequence, give a recommended option when useful, and continue independent work.

A missing technical implementation detail is not itself a missing product decision.
